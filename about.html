About Light a Torch Foundation

Light a Torch Foundation is a community-centered nonprofit organization dedicated to improving the lives of vulnerable individuals across Jamaica. We believe every person deserves access to education, empowerment opportunities, and meaningful support that helps them reach their full potential.

Our Mission

Our mission is to shine a guiding light in places where it is needed most. Through scholarships, mentorship, skills training, and targeted donation programs, we help individuals and families build stronger, more secure futures.

Our Core Values

  • Empowerment — equipping individuals with tools to uplift themselves and their communities.
  • Equity — ensuring underserved groups gain access to resources and support systems.
  • Integrity — operating with full transparency, responsibility, and respect.

Our Work Across Jamaica

We collaborate with schools, youth groups, churches, and community leaders to identify and support those most at risk of being left behind. Our programs serve students, single-parent households, and young adults in need of guidance, skills, and opportunities.

With the support of donors and volunteers, Light a Torch Foundation continues to illuminate pathways to a brighter, more empowered Jamaica.
