Programs

How we improve lives on the ground

Our focus is simple: remove barriers that prevent vulnerable groups from accessing education, skills, and opportunities. Your support fuels programs that are practical, community-led, and designed for long-term change.

Education & School Support

We help students stay in school by supporting with exam fees, books, transportation, uniforms, and tutoring for key subjects.

Example: Your donation can help a student sit CSEC exams they otherwise could not afford.

Skills & Empowerment

Young adults and parents access training in areas like digital skills, small business management, and vocational trades to improve their income.

Example: A short skills course can be the first step away from unemployment or dependency.

Emergency & Family Support

For families facing crisis, we step in with food baskets, hygiene kits, school supplies, and targeted help identified through community partners.

Example: A single donation can help stabilize a household long enough for them to rebuild.
Impact

Your giving lights real lives

Behind every number is a face, a family, and a story of renewed hope. We work closely with local partners so support goes where it is needed most.

When you give to Light a Torch Foundation, you’re not just giving money—you’re opening doors. A student is able to sit an exam, a young person learns a skill that leads to employment, and a family facing hardship feels seen and supported.

Our commitment is to remain transparent, community-centered, and focused on long-term change. We regularly update supporters on the projects they’ve helped make possible.

Donate

Fuel education, opportunity & hope

Whether you give once or monthly, your support directly strengthens programs that uplift vulnerable people across Jamaica.

Your donation helps us:

  • Fund scholarships and exam fees for students in need.
  • Support skills training and empowerment workshops.
  • Provide care packages and emergency support to families in crisis.
  • Strengthen community hubs that are safe spaces for learning and growth.

We are committed to keeping administrative costs low so that the majority of funds go directly into programs. Transparent reporting and impact updates help you see where your support goes.
