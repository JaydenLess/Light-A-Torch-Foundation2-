Programs

How we improve lives on the ground

Our work focuses on practical, locally grounded initiatives that give vulnerable groups the tools, confidence, and support they need to move toward long-term stability and independence.

Education

Scholarships & School Support

We provide back-to-school grants, exam fees, transportation stipends, and school supplies for students from low-income households, so finances never block their potential.

Focus: Primary & secondary students in Jamaica.
Empowerment

Skills Training & Microenterprise

From digital literacy to small business management, our workshops equip youth and caregivers with practical skills to create sustainable income streams.

Focus: Unemployed youth and single parents.
Youth

Mentorship & Safe Spaces

We connect young people with positive role models, study groups, and safe community hubs where they can learn, ask questions, and build healthy friendships.

Focus: At-risk teens and school-leavers.
Care & Relief

Care Packages & Emergency Support

When families face a crisis—job loss, illness, or sudden hardship—we step in with food baskets, hygiene supplies, and short-term financial assistance.

Focus: Vulnerable households in partner communities across the island.
Impact

Stories behind every donation

We measure our success not just in numbers, but in transformed lives—students who graduate, parents who find stable income, and young people who rediscover their worth.

Light a Torch Foundation works hand in hand with schools, churches, and community leaders in Jamaica to identify those who are most at risk of being left behind. Our support goes beyond a single handout—we walk with families over time.

  • Community-based identification of vulnerable households and youth.
  • Regular follow-up visits and coaching with partner social workers.
  • Monitoring of school attendance and academic progress for supported students.
  • Feedback from participants to continually shape better programs.
