Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

Light a Torch Foundation (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website or participate in our programs.

Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Full name and contact information
  • Email addresses for newsletter subscriptions or volunteer forms
  • Donation-related information (amount, date, method)
  • Messages submitted through online forms

We do not collect or store credit/debit card details. All payment information is processed securely by third-party platforms such as PayPal or Donorbox.

How We Use Your Information

  • To acknowledge and process donations
  • To contact volunteers and respond to inquiries
  • To send newsletters or updates (if you opt-in)
  • To improve our website and services

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may share limited data with trusted service providers for:

  • Email delivery
  • Donation processing
  • Website analytics

Your Rights

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Unsubscribe from communications at any time
